MyVoice Lexicon

A communication aid for speech that recognizers struggle with. Instead of a bigger model, it shrinks the output space: a personal lexicon of the phrases a speaker actually uses reranks the recognizer's n-best list.

What v0.1.0 is, plainly: the recognizer is a text simulator. You type the words the speaker tried to say, pick how garbled the simulated recognizer is, and the lexicon reranker does its work on a simulated n-best list. No audio is recorded or recognized anywhere in this version. Speech playback is a placeholder tone, not a synthesized voice.
